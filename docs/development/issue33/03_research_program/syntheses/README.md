# Targeted Synthesis Artefact Guide

**Status:** PRELIMINARY  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

## Purpose

A targeted synthesis explains what may responsibly be concluded about one exact claim or tightly bounded problem family. It is derived from canonical registers and cannot create evidence or assessment status by narrative assertion.

## Naming and identity

- File: SYN-####_<short-slug>.md
- Directory: this directory
- ID, version, path, linked claims and candidates: recorded in synthesis_register.csv
- New files must be created through the project research-document scaffold.
- Every version states its Protocol version, related workstream IDs, claim IDs, candidate IDs, assessor, and review status.

## Required sections

1. **Exact question and bounded claim**
2. **Why a targeted synthesis was required**
3. **Pre-frozen mechanism, affected interests, boundaries, null hypothesis, and alternative explanations**
4. **Source-selection and screening method**
5. **Supporting evidence**
6. **Qualifying evidence and heterogeneity**
7. **Counterevidence, null findings, and counterevidence state**
8. **Benefits, legitimate purposes, and successful mitigations**
9. **Causal assessment**
10. **Empirical evidence status**
11. **Formulation status**
12. **Normative recognition as a separate axis**
13. **Materiality and rootness assessment where applicable**
14. **Equality-impact assessment where applicable**
15. **Applicability and limits**
16. **Justified wording**
17. **Prohibited stronger wording**
18. **Topology implications**
19. **Remaining GAP IDs**
20. **Review findings, responses, and residual risk**

## Traceability requirements

Every factual or normative source contribution cites an EVL identifier. Current claim, materiality, rootness, and equality assessments cite ASM, MAT, ROT, and EQA identifiers as applicable. Candidate and topology implications cite KAND and TOP identifiers. The prose may explain these records but not replace them.

## Review boundary

The pre-human evidence-derived synthesis and topology implications are versioned before founder or stakeholder adjustment. Human changes are logged and both pre-adjustment and post-adjustment objects remain reviewable.

A synthesis is not determination. It may recommend retain, merge, reclassify, hold, correct, or reject, but permanent constitutional status requires the later determination gates and human approval.

## Completion

A synthesis may move from DRAFT through IN_REVIEW to PILOT_PROVISIONAL or VERIFIED only when:

- included sources and screening flow reconcile;
- source dependencies and independence are assessed;
- counterevidence state is not NOT_YET_SEARCHED;
- exact wording and uncertainty are recorded;
- equality screening is complete where applicable;
- no open Critical review finding remains;
- material Major findings are resolved or explicitly risk-accepted by authorised decision.
