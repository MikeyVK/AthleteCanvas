# Issue 33 Research Glossary

**Status:** PRELIMINARY  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Purpose:** Provide one canonical location for operational definitions used in Issue 33 research.

## Rules

- **Approved** definitions are backed by an approved Decision Log entry.
- **Working** definitions support research but remain open to pilot revision.
- A definition with normative consequences cannot become Approved solely through repeated use.
- Related documents reference these terms rather than silently redefining them.
- [FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md](FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md) is the authoritative full contract for the DEC-012 terms indexed below. If an index entry loses a boundary or qualification, the full contract governs.

## Approved definitions

### Equality

- **Category:** Normative and constitutional
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** Every person possesses unconditional equal moral worth and the same fundamental digital rights, protection, and legal standing. Differences in implementation are permitted only when demonstrably necessary to make those same rights effectively accessible. They must never reduce the content, protection threshold, or contestability of those rights.
- **Includes:** Equal moral standing, equal fundamental rights, equal minimum protection, equal contestability, and narrowly justified accessibility measures.
- **Excludes:** Guaranteed identical outcomes; arbitrary privilege; stereotypes; cultural or circumstantial exceptions that lower rights.
- **Authority:** DEC-004

### Differentiated implementation

- **Category:** Normative and methodological
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** A variation in delivery or support that preserves the same right, goal, content, protection threshold, and contestability, and passes every safeguard in DEC-004.
- **Includes:** Evidence-based accessibility measures and delivery adaptations needed to make the same right usable.
- **Excludes:** Different classes of rights, permanent unreviewable privileges, forced cultural assignment, and reduced protection.
- **Authority:** DEC-004

### Founder moral ground

- **Category:** Normative
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** Equal human moral worth; technology as a means that serves life; foundational technological benefit independent of privilege or background; and sustainable balance between people, their environments, and Earth.
- **Includes:** The moral lens used to judge constitutional relevance.
- **Excludes:** Empirical diagnoses, architectural choices, and claims that a specific intervention works.
- **Authority:** DEC-003

### Internet

- **Category:** Research boundary
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** The shared communication infrastructure through which connected systems exchange data using interoperable network protocols.
- **Caution:** Connectivity and reach do not prescribe one data, application, governance, or ownership architecture.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Digital data architecture

- **Category:** Research object
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** The set of technically enforceable structures, rules, protocols, and assignments of authority that determine how digital data is formed, identified, stored, copied, combined, derived, exchanged, changed, and terminated throughout its lifecycle.
- **Includes:** The representation and persistence of human authority, rights, powers, duties, and responsibilities through relevant data operations and system boundaries.
- **Excludes:** Law, business models, institutions, social norms, or individual conduct by themselves.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Factual societal situation

- **Category:** Empirical
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** An observable state or event described without first calling it desirable, harmful, reasonable, unfair, or unacceptable.
- **Required fields:** Conditions, involved parties or interests, occurrence, and directly observable consequence.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Societal problem

- **Category:** Empirical and normative
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** A factual societal situation whose consequence conflicts with an explicit, Approved human norm.
- **Caution:** The factual situation and the human norm remain separately visible and separately authorised.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Human norm

- **Category:** Normative
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** An explicit human choice about the state that must hold, recorded independently from empirical evidence status.
- **Required fields:** Subject, object, required state, scope, explicit exceptions, authority, and status.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Digital mechanism

- **Category:** Empirical and architectural
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** A repeatable relation of technical operations, rules, or dependencies through which a factual societal situation arises, expands, or persists.
- **Caution:** A technology label such as platform, AI, database, or cloud is not by itself a mechanism.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Architectural contribution

- **Category:** Empirical and architectural
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** An evidenced relation through which an architectural property facilitates, incentivises, amplifies, entrenches, or necessitates a named digital mechanism.
- **Excludes:** Mere permission without a demonstrated contribution.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

### Architectural deficit

- **Category:** Architectural
- **Status:** Approved
- **Definition:** A property or missing foundational rule of a digital data architecture through which a digital mechanism is facilitated, incentivised, amplified, entrenched, or necessitated across systems or implementations.
- **Required challenge:** Identify the expected change under an alternative foundational rule while retaining the Internet as communication infrastructure.
- **Authority:** DEC-012 and `FOUNDATIONAL_DEFINITIONS.md` 0.1

## Working methodological definitions

All Working definitions below predate DEC-012 and require compatibility review before pilot approval. They may refine an Approved term for a bounded method, but may not redefine or weaken the Approved foundational contract.

### Sociotechnical Internet

- **Category:** Research object
- **Status:** Superseded before approval
- **Definition:** The interacting system formed by physical and ecological infrastructure; protocols and technical architecture; applications, platforms, and interfaces; data, algorithmic, and AI systems; ownership, market, and revenue models; and law, governance, and social institutions.
- **Historical use:** May remain as a descriptive context model, but is not the primary causal research object or an admission ground for problem candidates.
- **Authority:** DEC-005 was Proposed and is replaced by Approved DEC-012.


### Exact claim

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** One bounded proposition whose subject, relationship or effect, population or affected interest, context, period where material, and uncertainty can be evaluated against evidence.
- **Distinguish from:** Topic, candidate title, source conclusion, and problem family.

### Candidate

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A temporary phenomenon retained for investigation without presuming that it is an independent, evidenced, or constitutionally relevant problem.
- **Distinguish from:** Permanent problem ID and approved problem family.

### Root problem

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** An in-scope structural condition with a traceable causal path to fundamental harm or unjustified material risk, causal upstreamness across multiple manifestations or a recurring class, persistence beyond one mitigated manifestation, explanatory gain over a parent condition, and resistance to more precise non-lossy reduction.
- **Required challenge:** Record a countermodel describing what would be observed if the condition were not root-level.
- **Caution:** A broad label does not become root-level through severity language, examples, or citation count.

### Mechanism

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** The process or causal pathway through which a structural condition changes the probability, distribution, magnitude, or persistence of a benefit or harm.
- **Caution:** A plausible mechanism is not proof that the claimed effect occurs at material scale.

### Consequence

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** An outcome or changed condition produced or amplified through one or more mechanisms.
- **Caution:** The same phenomenon may be a consequence in one relationship and a mechanism in another.

### Affected interest

- **Category:** Methodological and normative
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A human, collective, institutional, ecological, or intergenerational interest that may benefit, be burdened, or be placed at risk.
- **Distinguish from:** The person, group, institution, community, ecosystem, or future generation that holds or embodies the interest.

### Fundamental harm

- **Category:** Methodological and normative
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A materially adverse effect or unjustified material risk connected through a digitally mediated structural mechanism to an explicitly identified protected interest.
- **Assessment dimensions:** Severity, scale, duration and recurrence, reversibility, unequal externalisation, loss of agency or contestability, systemic spillover, and ecological or intergenerational persistence.
- **Pilot anchors:** Each dimension uses its frozen HIGH, MODERATE, and LOW anchor in Research Protocol section 8.1; UNKNOWN and NOT_APPLICABLE have distinct semantics and require reasons.
- **Pilot threshold:** At least one HIGH or two MODERATE materiality dimensions, plus structural recurrence and processed counterevidence, makes a candidate eligible for further consideration rather than constitutionally determined.
- **Protected-interest states:** Approved, Normatively Recognised, Proposed, or Not Identified.

### Constitutional relevance

- **Category:** Methodological and normative
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** Durable governance significance within Ypsia's potential constitutional scope: the evidenced condition is sufficiently persistent, cross-actor, cross-application, power-shaping, or resistant to ordinary product-level handling that later constitutional deliberation is warranted.
- **Caution:** This research-stage classification does not establish the need for a particular right, prohibition, mandate, governance rule, or architecture.
- **Approval rule:** Human approval is required.

### Source

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** An identifiable and traceable publication, dataset, legal instrument, standard, artefact, or other record evaluated for a specific contribution to one or more exact claims.
- **Caution:** A search result, generated summary, or unverified quotation is not a durable source record.

### Evidence relationship

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A claim-bound record explaining how a specific source supports, qualifies, contradicts, or contextualises an exact claim, including locator, limitations, independence, and applicability.
- **Distinguish from:** A bibliography entry without claim linkage.

### Counterevidence

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** Credible evidence that contradicts, narrows, conditions, reverses, or offers a serious alternative explanation for a claim.
- **Caution:** A contrary opinion or weak source is not automatically counterevidence.

### Independent evidence

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** Evidence whose relevant data, analytic origin, authorship or institutional incentives, and inferential pathway are sufficiently distinct that it provides more than repeated reporting of the same evidentiary basis.
- **Caution:** Two publications, citations, or organisations do not necessarily provide two independent evidence grounds.

### Normative recognition

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A separate assessment of whether an interest, duty, risk, or right is Authoritatively, Convergently, Limitedly, or Contestedly recognised in applicable legal, ethical, political, or institutional frameworks; whether none was identified; whether recognition is Not Applicable; or whether it remains Not Assessed.
- **Caution:** Normative recognition may coexist with any empirical evidence status and does not establish prevalence, magnitude, or causality.

### Empirical evidence status

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A bounded synthesis judgement under Protocol 0.1: Established High Confidence, Convergent Conditional, Contested Mixed, Plausible Understudied, Insufficient Inconclusive, Refuted, Not Applicable, or Not Assessed.
- **Caution:** The status applies to one exact claim within recorded boundaries; it is not a general source score or vote count.

### Formulation status

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** Assessment of whether claim wording is Adequately Bounded, Needs Narrowing, Materially Misformulated, Corrected, or Not Assessed.
- **Caution:** Misformulation and empirical refutation are distinct outcomes.

### Workstream

- **Category:** Project and methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A bounded research unit with its own questions, provisionally proposed six-layer scope, affected interests, search record, screening decisions, evidence synthesis, and completion status.

### Search iteration

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** One predeclared query batch covering the specified source classes, concepts and synonyms, plus at least one counterevidence or mitigation path, with its identity, linked searches, flow counts, and novelty yield recorded in search_iterations.csv.
- **Excludes:** Trivial wording variants counted as separate progress.

### Research phase

- **Category:** Project
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** The substantive period in which the problem landscape and its evidence are investigated before constitutional derivation or Charter drafting.
- **Distinguish from:** The PGMCP workflow metadata value. For this branch, research is intentionally retained even though it is not a declared phase of the docs workflow.
- **Authority:** DEC-001 and DEC-007

### Conceptual saturation

- **Category:** Methodological
- **Status:** Working
- **Definition:** A provisional workstream state reached only after the logged stopping rule in the Research Protocol is satisfied.
- **Excludes:** Proof of completeness, empirical sufficiency, or permanent closure.
