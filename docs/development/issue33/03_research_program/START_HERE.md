## Authority order

When documents conflict, apply this order:

1. repository instructions and the explicitly active role;
2. Approved entries in DECISION_LOG.md;
3. an approved and frozen Research Charter;
4. an approved and frozen Research Protocol;
5. canonical structured [registers](registers/README.md);
6. reviewed workstream syntheses and review records;
7. current founder concepts as normative or hypothesis context;
8. recovered historical artefacts and transfer documents.

A Draft or Proposed document does not outrank an Approved decision. Human approval cannot upgrade empirical evidence.

- canonical registers contain every durable formal Protocol record created;

- Approved decisions are in DECISION_LOG.md;
- STATUS names exactly one active object and one next action;
- files are versioned by an authorised workflow role, or STATUS explicitly says that the checkpoint remains uncommitted;
- the next session begins from this file rather than the historical top-level resume instructions.
